Proposed terms of use

These preparation notes require operator review and completion before launch. They do not offer an operational service or establish finalized service commitments.

Intended use

Root Cause Studio is planned as an internal workflow to send selected finished content to an authorized TikTok account’s inbox. The account owner would complete the post in TikTok. This site does not currently connect accounts, receive files, or upload content.

Content and account authorization

The proposed workflow is intended for accounts the operator is authorized to manage and content the operator has permission to use. The implementation will need to present the selected content for confirmation before sending it to TikTok.

Platform dependencies

Any integration would depend on TikTok permissions, availability, technical limits, and applicable platform rules. Sandbox testing does not establish production approval. No approval, delivery, publication, uptime, or continued API availability is promised in this draft.

Review TikTok’s terms, TikTok’s app review guidelines, and GitHub’s terms for the relevant external services.

Items to finalize

Operator identity and contact details, permitted access, finalized data handling, support and termination arrangements, and any other applicable service terms must be reviewed before launch. See the draft privacy notice for unresolved data handling details.
